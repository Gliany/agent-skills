# Assets, depreciation and loans

- **Acquisition.** Establish the asset category, purchase and in-service dates, supported cost and directly attributable costs. Debit the asset for cost including permanently irrecoverable VAT where applicable. Debit supported recoverable input VAT separately and credit payable or bank. Tie the asset register to the acquisition journal.
- **Depreciation.** Separate book depreciation policy and useful life from tax depreciation and deductions. For a tax calculation verify the period's applicable regulations and precise asset class. Personal computers, servers, other electronic equipment, vehicle classes and specialized machinery can have different treatments. Check leasehold improvements against the actual lease and applicable book and tax rules. Avoid a flat presumed rate. Use the supported depreciable base, in-service date, first-period convention and method. For a straight-line policy apply the period fraction to that base and cap cumulative depreciation at the depreciable amount. Debit depreciation expense and credit accumulated depreciation. Reconcile opening cost and accumulated depreciation, additions, disposals and closing balances. For tax schedules use the applicable year's instructions and supporting fixed-asset form, such as Form 1342 where relevant. [Tax Authority annual forms by year](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/topics/annual-tax-reports-for-itc).
- **Borrowing.** A drawdown normally debits cash and credits the loan liability. Split a repayment between principal, interest and evidenced fees using the lender schedule, with any prior interest accrual cleared against its liability. Verify foreign-currency rates and treatment where relevant. Reconcile outstanding principal to the statement. Loan proceeds and principal repayments belong in the liability and cash movement, separate from operating income and expenses.
