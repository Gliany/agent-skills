# Document, VAT and settlement controls

Verify applicable law and Tax Authority instructions for the entity, document and period before applying a rate, deadline, exemption, tax point, or deduction. For ordinary exempt-dealer transactions, record sales without output VAT and purchases gross without input-VAT recovery. Flag unusual asset or real-estate sales for entity-specific review. [Tax Authority VAT guide](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/vat-to-the-new-dealer?chapterIndex=4). Keep the bookkeeping basis distinct from the VAT tax point.

## Posting patterns

- **Sales and receipts.** For a recognized credit sale, debit receivables and credit revenue plus any output VAT due at the confirmed tax point. On settlement, debit bank or clearing and credit receivables for the amount settled. If a source combines invoice and receipt, represent both events once. A cash-basis service or partial payment may have a different VAT timing from an accrual sale. Check the tax point and the effect of any tax invoice already issued. Reconcile partial payments, credit notes, refunds, and chargebacks against the original record.
- **Purchases and input VAT.** Credit the supplier or settlement account for gross cost. Debit the expense or asset and book only supported, recoverable VAT to input VAT. Confirm supplier tax invoice or import evidence, eligible use, and any restriction. Review passenger-car acquisition separately from operating costs, employee benefits separately from refreshments or hospitality, and VAT recovery separately from income-tax deductibility. If deduction is pending, use the entity's approved pending treatment and exclude it from recoverable VAT. Include permanently irrecoverable VAT in the corresponding expense or asset cost as applicable.
- **VAT clearing.** Reconcile eligible input and output VAT to the approved period totals, then clear to VAT payable or receivable. A later payment or refund settles that balance. Keep draft clearing separate from filing.
- **Withholding at source.** Where withholding applies, verify the certificate and rate for the supplier, payment type, and payment date. At settlement, debit supplier payable for the amount discharged and credit bank for net cash and withholding payable for the difference. Clear the withholding on evidenced remittance. For tax withheld by a customer, separately track the supported credit/receivable and net cash against the gross receivable. [Tax Authority certificates](https://www.gov.il/he/service/itc-gmishurim).

## Israel Invoices

Check the invoice's issue date and **amount before VAT**, the actual VAT treatment, and the recipient's VAT status. Apply the official threshold for that date using a strict greater-than comparison when the rule says exceeds. Verify the historical commencement and any deferrals for 2024 invoices before deciding. Do not use today's threshold for historical invoices.

Distinguish the seller's duty on a qualifying buyer request from the buyer's deduction condition. A missing buyer request does not establish eligibility to deduct input VAT. An absent or unverified required number does not by itself void the invoice. Flag the affected input VAT for review and exclude it from recoverable VAT while required evidence is missing. Check the official procedure before assuming how the supplier can correct it or which exception applies. Separately flag the expense for income-tax review where a required allocation number is missing. A bookkeeping expense entry does not establish deductibility under section 32(18). [Tax Authority circular on income-tax deductibility](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/professional-directives-090226-1/he/IncomeTax_professional-directives-090226-1.pdf).

Preserve the source allocation identifier as text, including leading zeroes, distinct from the invoice serial number. Handle the displayed nine-digit representation and the full API identifier according to the destination specification. Length alone proves neither validity nor a mismatch. Match evidence to the invoice. An allocation confirmation for a future transaction is distinct from an allocated number for the issued tax invoice. [Tax Authority implementation instruction](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-071225-1/he/vat_inst-071225-1.pdf), [invoice verification guidance](https://www.gov.il/he/service/verify-vendor-invoice-information), [Israel Invoices FAQ](https://www.gov.il/en/pages/faq_israel_invoice).

## Exports

A review CSV or XLSX is a working paper. For an actual bookkeeping-system import, use that system's schema and validate the mappings, dates, signs, decimal fields and identifiers. Keep OPENFORMAT/BKMV ledger export, PCN874 detailed VAT and annual Form 6111 mapping distinct. Verify the format version and period-specific applicability before labeling a file as conforming. [Uniform-format guidance](https://www.gov.il/he/service/registration-software-designed-managing-computerized-accounting-system), [PCN874](https://www.gov.il/he/service/detailed-vat-reporting), [Form 6111](https://www.gov.il/he/service/itc6111).
