# Payroll journals

Start from an approved payroll register or payroll system export and the entity's payroll-to-ledger mapping. Preserve the pay period, accrual date, actual payment dates and remittance references. Record only applicable employee deductions, employer costs and benefit components. Treat an unapproved calculation as a draft estimate and do not fill a missing deduction with a guessed figure.

- **National and health insurance.** If verifying a calculation, use the official employee category and corresponding Form 102 column for that period. Residency, age, controlling shareholder status, receipt of an old-age pension, and disability certification can affect the row. Confirm the insurable base, reduced-band ceiling, maximum insurable income and each employee/employer component. Apply the reduced and full rates to their respective slices of the confirmed base up to the cap and match the payroll rounding policy. Confirm special categories and adjustments from the official table. [National Insurance employee rate tables](https://www.btl.gov.il/ENGLISH%20HOMEPAGE/INSURANCE/RATESANDAMOUNT/Pages/forSalaried.aspx).
- **Tax withholding, benefits and contributions.** Use approved employee income-tax withholding. Check the applicable agreement, eligibility, bases, rates and ceilings for employee and employer pension, severance, and optional קרן השתלמות. Where applicable separate taxable benefits and avoid counting a noncash benefit twice in the net-pay bridge. Use approved entitlement and rate for דמי הבראה. For סעיף 14 check the documented arrangement, deposits, and any remaining employer obligation. Keep deposits payable and any residual provision distinct where applicable.
- **Accrual.** Debit gross wages and applicable employer costs. Credit approved net wages payable and the applicable withholding and benefit liabilities, with employee and employer portions traceable separately. Check gross-to-net against the payroll register and total liabilities against the remittance schedules.
- **Settlement.** Debit wage payable and credit bank at actual payroll payment. Clear tax, insurance and fund liabilities against their own evidenced remittances. Reconcile amounts and periods before treating a difference as a posting error.
