# Israeli reporting checks

Use only the checks relevant to the requested draft. Record each applied rule, its effective date, the official source, and unresolved applicability. If the applicable rule or a material document cannot be verified, separate affected amounts and leave the final statutory total or deadline unconfirmed as appropriate.

## VAT

- Confirm registration, cadence and filing channel for the entity and period. Check period boundaries, extensions, and whether detailed reporting applies. Use an annual turnover schedule when preparing an עוסק פטור declaration. Use a periodic output and input VAT schedule for an applicable registered filer. [Exempt-dealer declaration](https://www.gov.il/he/service/vat-declarationisexempt), [periodic VAT reports](https://www.gov.il/he/service/reporting-or-payment-of-vat-reports), [detailed VAT reporting](https://www.gov.il/he/service/detailed-vat-reporting).
- Reconcile taxable sales and credits to their source documents and the applicable tax point. Separate standard-rated, zero-rated, and exempt lines. Reconcile output VAT to the source documents and the rate in force. [VAT rates](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/vat-rate-amount-new).
- Calculate deductible input VAT from the VAT shown on valid supplier tax invoices and import documentation. Check credits, the deductible share, and restrictions such as mixed use or vehicle expenses. Separate equipment input VAT from other input VAT where the reporting form calls for it. A purchase total, receipt, or pro forma invoice alone does not establish deductible input VAT.
- Where Israel Invoices rules apply, check the pre-VAT threshold in force on the invoice date and verify that the required allocation number is evidenced and matches the invoice. Keep affected VAT out of the verified deductible subtotal if evidence is missing. Document any applicable exception separately with its basis. [Israel Invoices effective dates](https://www.gov.il/en/pages/faq_israel_invoice), [allocation-number service and applicability](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-assignment-number-for-tax-invoice), [supplier invoice verification guidance](https://www.gov.il/he/service/verify-vendor-invoice-information).
- Show supported output VAT minus supported deductible input VAT, with rejected or pending items and their impact separately. For PCN874 working papers verify the period's official fields and any permitted aggregation. Keep document type, invoice number, relevant date, net amount, VAT, and counterparty reference traceable. Mask full identifiers in general summaries.

## Income-tax working papers

- Separate accounting profit from a tax computation and show the adjustment bridge. Apply corporate rates, personal brackets or surtax only after confirming the entity, tax year, applicable official instructions, and required inputs. Keep income-tax advances and National Insurance distinct from VAT. [Israel Tax Authority](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/israel_tax_authority), [National Insurance Institute](https://www.btl.gov.il).
- For a confirmed בעל עסק זעיר track, verify eligibility and the year's instructions before using its deemed-expense deduction in the tax schedule. Keep actual expenses in the management P&L and avoid combining the deduction with actual expenses. [Israel Tax Authority's small-business track](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/small-business-owner-income-tax).
- For Form 6111 working papers, use the tax year's instructions, verify applicability, and map the P&L, tax adjustments, and balance sheet to that year's codes. An ordinary chart of accounts is a source for mapping. Keep this annex and the annual corporate registry report distinct. [Form 6111](https://www.gov.il/he/service/itc6111).
