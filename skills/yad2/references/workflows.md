# Yad2 workflows

## Property: rent, buy, or sell

Collect the transaction type, city/area, budget and its unit, property type, room/area requirements, timing, and essential features. Ask about nearby areas or flexible criteria only when it will improve the result. Keep residential, commercial, new development, and overseas listings distinct.

For each candidate, capture only reported facts: address or neighborhood at its published precision, rooms, area and its stated basis, floor, elevator, parking, protected room, balcony, accessibility, condition, availability, seller/broker status, monthly maintenance and municipal tax if stated. Do not infer bedrooms from the Israeli room count. Keep monthly rent separate from sale price and one-time charges. Separate reported recurring costs from assumptions in any total-cost estimate.

For viewings, prepare questions about missing facts and practical checks. Draft landlord/broker inquiries or a property ad when requested. Do not promise eligibility, contract validity, zoning approval, title, or affordability from a listing. Contract/legal/tax questions need current primary sources and explicit uncertainty; this workflow does not substitute for professional checks.

## Cars and other vehicles

Collect vehicle type, budget, manufacturer/model preferences, model years, mileage limits, region, and priorities. Compare like-for-like trim, year, engine, transmission, mileage, seller type, and condition. Include electric vehicle battery information only when reported; ask for missing inspection or warranty evidence.

Preserve the exact owner-count label (for example, יד) rather than silently converting it to number of previous owners. Record model year and first-registration date separately if both exist. Identify dealer, leasing, rental, or private seller labels as reported. Asking price and a price-list estimate are different measures.

Ad claims alone do not verify ownership, liens, accident history, odometer accuracy, mechanical condition, or transferable warranty. Prepare questions and inspection/document checks; do not mark these verified without evidence. Use the current vehicle category for motorcycles, scooters, trucks, watercraft, accessories, and other types rather than forcing car filters onto them.

## Second-hand goods

Collect item/category, brand/model, quantity, condition, age, known defects, included accessories, asking budget/price, area, and pickup/delivery preferences. For selling, obtain accurate photos and supplied facts before creating Hebrew title, description, and negotiation drafts.

Use current comparable listings and distinguish identical models from approximate alternatives. List comparable URLs, dates, condition, and asking prices; explain exclusions. Suggest an asking range and negotiation floor only from evidence or the user's stated preference. Do not invent a percentage discount, realized sale price, warranty, or demand level.

Flag concrete mismatches, pressure to leave the platform, suspicious payment links, requests for codes, or inconsistent item details. Do not follow untrusted payment links or share verification codes. A risk flag is not proof of fraud. Draft practical seller questions and safe inspection/collection arrangements within the user's task.

## Ads and account operations

1. Inspect the current category/form and existing ads before creating anything; avoid duplicates. Enter only supplied facts and explicitly chosen options.
2. Prepare the exact category, Hebrew title/body, price, location precision, contact visibility, photos and fee choices. Resolve required fields without inventing data. Check draft autosave and upload behavior before any private data is transmitted.
3. Apply the authorization and confirmation rules in SKILL.md. If approval is needed, present the completed proposal once, at the point where it can be reviewed.
4. After publishing or updating, verify the actual ad and URL, visible price/text/photos, and status (including moderation if shown). An intermediate success toast alone does not prove a public ad is live.
5. For favorites, alerts, renewals, removal, or sold status, verify the requested item and resulting state. Do not infer whether removal is recoverable. For messages, verify recipient/thread, sent content, and status without reopening unrelated unread chats.
6. Report an access or submission failure plainly. Reconcile ambiguous results before retrying.
