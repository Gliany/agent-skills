# Extended business operations

These are operational workflows, not guarantees of feature availability. Business App, Web, Desktop and Platform differ by account, region, rollout, device and permissions. Observe the current interface before offering execution. If an operation is phone-only or unavailable, prepare exact content and a short manual handoff; do not claim completion.

## Business identity and storefront

- Inspect profile name, category, description, address, hours/timezone, website, email and image. Prepare a before/after change list; save only authorized values and re-open to verify. Never infer an address or opening hours from old conversations.
- Inspect catalogs, item details and collections where available. Create/update products from user-approved names, descriptions, item codes, prices/currency, links and images. Confirm item identity and existing values before replacing anything. Hide/remove only explicitly selected items. Distinguish saved, pending review, approved and publicly visible.
- Draft or send requested catalog/item links through the outbound transaction. A catalog publication and sharing its link are separate actions.
- Inspect incoming carts/order requests and summarize item, quantity, options, customer question and missing information. Confirm availability/pricing from an approved source before making commitments. Do not mark orders paid, issue invoices, accept payments or promise delivery merely because a WhatsApp message says so.
- Inspect business short links/public contact QR codes when requested. A public business QR differs from a login QR; verify the actual destination/number before sharing. Publishing a link elsewhere requires destination authorization.

## Customer service and conversation organization

- Identify leads, quote requests, complaints, bookings, supplier questions, requested documents and overdue commitments within the inspected scope. Propose next actions; do not invent CRM state or assignees.
- Reuse existing labels and meanings. For create/rename/merge/remove requests, show the affected labels and chats first when scope is ambiguous. A label change is not proof that a payment, refund or delivery occurred.
- Support requested archiving, unarchiving, pinning, starring, muting and unread reminders where exposed. Do not interpret "review" as permission to reorganize or silence alerts.
- Read quick replies; draft additions or improvements. Save only authorized shortcut, body and attachments, checking for collisions. Resolve placeholders before a send. Business App quick replies and Platform approved message templates are different objects.
- Configure greeting/away messages only with approved text, eligible recipients, exclusions, schedule and timezone. Inspect existing rules first; do not enable overlapping rules that can double-reply. An approved rule is standing authorization only within its defined bounds.
- For metrics, report only counts derived from a clearly stated observed period and population. Do not calculate company-wide response performance from a partial sidebar or equate read receipts with customer satisfaction.

## Message, group and account administration

- Support authorized quoted replies, mentions, reactions, editing, forwarding and deletion when available. Verify the exact source message. A reaction or vote is an external communication; forwarding additionally discloses source content.
- For groups, inspect membership/admin rights when relevant. Create groups, invite/add/remove participants, change subject/image/description, alter permissions or promote/demote admins only with exact authorization. Confirm participants and visibility of phone numbers/content. Do not expose or reset invitation links without instruction.
- For polls, events, contact cards, location, Status or channel posts, verify support, final content and audience. Live location requires explicit duration and stop scope. Do not initiate calls or record/send voice without authorization; do not answer calls automatically.
- Distinguish archive, clear, delete for me and delete for everyone. Identify exact items and consequences before destructive action; never fall back to another deletion mode. Do not bypass expired edit/delete controls.
- Treat block, unblock, report, leave group, contact edits, notification/privacy/security changes, linked-device changes and logout as explicit account actions. Reporting may disclose content to WhatsApp; do not bundle it into blocking automatically. Never change number, migrate accounts, delete accounts or unlink devices as troubleshooting.
- Export/back up only with explicit scope and protected destination. Do not extract hidden app databases or authentication stores, bypass disappearing/view-once restrictions, or silently retain expiring media.

## Multiple recipients and campaigns

Prepare an exact deduplicated recipient list, individualized content and attachment set. Separate direct chats from groups. Obtain authorization for the concrete batch if not already approved. Respect opt-outs and current product restrictions; do not scrape contacts or use Web as an unsolicited bulk-send engine. Costs, scheduling, paid broadcasts, ads and subscriptions require explicit approval of scope and budget. For supported broadcasts, verify eligibility in the actual account and track each available outcome; a batch submission is not proof every recipient received it.
